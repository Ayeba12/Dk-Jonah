# DK Jonah Website Content

## Brand Summary

DK Jonah is a lifestyle and personal voice brand built around chronic illness, neurodiversity, faith, softness, and honest belonging. The website should feel like a quiet room for people who have carried invisible realities for too long.

The brand does not exist to fix people, diagnose people, or offer perfect answers. It exists to help people feel seen, less alone, and more able to name what they have been living through.

## Audience Profile

The primary audience is people who feel left behind by hustle culture, especially women and faith-aware readers navigating invisible illness, neurodivergence, emotional sensitivity, exhaustion, grief, or pressure to perform.

They may be carrying silent diagnoses, unnamed fatigue, or a sense that they are failing at something no one else can see. They want honesty, language, gentleness, faith without performance, and tools that respect their real capacity.

Core audience needs:

- To feel seen without having to explain everything.
- To read words that make their inner experience feel less strange.
- To find faith-based encouragement that does not shame them for struggling.
- To discover softer systems for living, resting, creating, and staying connected.
- To belong to a quiet community where slowness is not treated as failure.

## Core Message

You are not broken. You are not behind. You are not alone.

DK Jonah is a counter-message to shame, silence, and the pressure to get it together before being seen. The website should restore connection with self, spirit, and others through honesty, softness, and shared truth.

## Positioning

DK Jonah is a reflective lifestyle platform for people navigating invisible realities in a world built around visible performance. Through essays, resources, community notes, and faith-aware reflections, it offers language for what hurts, comfort for what feels lonely, and gentle tools for living with more grace.

## Voice And Tone

The voice should be:

- Soft but clear.
- Faith-aware but not preachy.
- Honest but not heavy for the sake of being heavy.
- Reflective, personal, and grounded.
- Warm, intimate, and community-minded.
- Encouraging without making medical, clinical, or guaranteed healing claims.

Avoid:

- Harsh productivity language.
- Overly polished self-help language.
- Medical promises.
- Shame-based faith language.
- Sales-heavy conversion copy.

## Design Direction

Keep the existing Avenzor design system structure and fonts.

Fonts must not change:

- Display: Avenzor Space Grotesk.
- Body/UI: Avenzor Inter.

Only the color direction should change.

Suggested color tokens:

- Canvas ivory: `#f8f2e8`
- Warm paper: `#fffaf2`
- Main text: `#111111`
- Soft charcoal: `#3a332b`
- Muted text: `#7a7065`
- Fine line: `#ded2c1`
- Subtle gold: `#b68a3a`
- Gold soft: `#ead9ad`
- Deep grounding tone: `#201a16`
- Soft section wash: `#f1e7d8`

Color usage:

- Use ivory and warm paper for the main background.
- Use black and soft charcoal for text.
- Use subtle gold for links, accents, calls to action, and focus states.
- Use the deep grounding tone for dark editorial bands.
- Do not introduce orange or blue as active brand colors.

## Content Safety Notes

- Do not promise healing, recovery, diagnosis, treatment, or guaranteed outcomes.
- Do not position DK Jonah as a medical, therapy, or crisis support service.
- Use gentle disclaimers where resources touch health, faith, or emotional struggle.
- Encourage professional support when appropriate.
- Keep faith language spacious and compassionate.
