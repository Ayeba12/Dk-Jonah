# What people search and ask, and where dkjonah.com answers

Written 3 October 2026. A working map of the questions people type into Google and Bing, and ask AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot), that line up with DK's work. For each cluster it says where the site answers today, what is missing, and what to build. It is a plan for DK to approve, not locked copy.

House rules apply to anything built from it: British spelling, NO GraGra, Quiet Focus as two words, tool names in full, no "free", no prices, no hype, no em dashes.

## How to read it

- **Search queries** are short phrases typed into Google or Bing. Rankings there depend on a page that answers the query in its own words, plus the title, the structured data and links.
- **AI questions** are full sentences asked to an assistant. Assistants answer from pages that define terms plainly, name the author, carry question-and-answer structure, and say the same thing everywhere. The FAQ page and llms.txt now do this; essays and tool pages can do more of it.
- **Where we answer today** names the page that currently holds the answer. **Gap** is what stops that page being the best answer on the internet. **Build** is the smallest thing that closes the gap.
- Priority: **A** build first, **B** next, **C** when there is time.

## What AI assistants reward, in one paragraph

A page gets cited when it states a definition in its first sentences, names the author with a real bio, uses the same term the same way on every page, carries question-and-answer structure the assistant can map to the user's question, and is linked from other pages the assistant already trusts. The site now does the first four for the FAQ and the essays. The missing piece is volume: more questions answered in DK's words, especially in clusters 4, 5 and 8 where few other people are writing.

## How we will know it is working

- Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools: queries that bring impressions, by page, checked monthly. The first signal is impressions for the branded terms and the long questions, not clicks.
- Ask the assistants directly once a month: "Who is DK Jonah?", "What is hidden captivity?", "Recommend a speaker on chronic illness and faith in the UK." Note whether dkjonah.com is cited and what it is cited for.
- The FAQ page's question-and-answer rich results in Google, once the page has been recrawled.
